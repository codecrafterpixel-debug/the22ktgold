Our Heritage & Craft

ROOTED IN MANEK CHOWK

Manufacturing Heritage with Modern Excellence.

For generations, Manek Chowk has been the beating heart of India's gold trade and Gujarati jewellery craftsmanship. THE 22KT GOLD is established directly within this heritage ecosystem, operating a dedicated manufacturing unit powered by over 25 master goldsmiths.

Unlike traditional retail jewelers who add heavy showroom markups and intermediary margins, we operate as an open workshop — giving you direct access to fine jewellery crafted exclusively in certified 22KT (916) gold.
